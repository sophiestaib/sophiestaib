Garbanzos con Espinacas — Ingredients & Instructions

Ingredients

  • 400g canned chickpeas (drained)
  • 200g fresh spinach
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, sliced
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 2 tbsp olive oil, salt & pepper

Instructions

  1. Warm oil in a pan. Sauté onion until translucent.
  2. Add garlic and paprika, cook 1 minute.
  3. Add chickpeas and a splash of water; simmer 5–7 min.
  4. Add spinach, cook until wilted. Season to taste and serve.

Pasta Primavera — Ingredients & Instructions

Ingredients

  • 300g pasta (penne or fusilli)
  • 1 bunch asparagus, trimmed
  • 1 zucchini, sliced
  • 150g cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 2 tbsp olive oil, lemon, salt, pepper
  • Fresh basil to finish

Instructions

  1. Cook pasta to al dente; reserve 1/2 cup pasta water.
  2. Sauté asparagus and zucchini in olive oil until tender-crisp.
  3. Toss vegetables with pasta, tomatoes, lemon juice and reserved water.
  4. Finish with basil, adjust seasoning and serve warm or chilled.

Zucchini Chickpeas Curry — Ingredients & Instructions

Ingredients

  • 2 medium zucchini, cubed
  • 1 can chickpeas, drained
  • 1 onion, chopped; 2 cloves garlic
  • 1 tbsp curry powder, 1 tsp cumin
  • 400ml coconut milk, salt, cilantro

Instructions

  1. Sauté onion and garlic until soft. Add spices and toast briefly.
  2. Add zucchini and chickpeas, stir to coat with spices.
  3. Pour in coconut milk, simmer until zucchini is tender and sauce thickens.
  4. Garnish with cilantro and serve with rice or flatbread.

Pumpkin Soup — Ingredients & Instructions

Ingredients

  • 600g pumpkin, peeled and diced
  • 1 onion, 2 cloves garlic
  • 600ml vegetable stock, 1 potato (optional for creaminess)
  • 2 tbsp olive oil, nutmeg, salt & pepper

Instructions

  1. Sauté onion and garlic in oil, add pumpkin (and potato) and cook briefly.
  2. Add stock, bring to a boil then simmer until soft (15–20 min).
  3. Blend until smooth, season, add a pinch of nutmeg and serve warm.
