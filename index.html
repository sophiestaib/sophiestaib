WHY EAT SEASONAL?

Making small adjustments to your shopping habits can significantly benefit the environment. Choosing seasonal, locally grown fruits and vegetables is one of the most effective ways to practice sustainability in daily life.

MINIMIZES ENERGY USE

Out-of-season produce often requires energy-intensive heated greenhouses to grow, or massive refrigeration systems to stay fresh during long journeys.

REDUCES CARBON FOOTPRINT

Food grown locally doesn't have to travel long distances by plane or truck, drastically cutting down on greenhouse gas emissions from transportation.

BETTER TASTE AND NUTRITION

Crops picked at peak ripe- ness are more flavorful and retain more vitamins than produce harvested early to ripen in transit.

SUPPORTS LOCAL ECONOMIES

Buying from nearby farmers keeps money within your community and helps preserve local farmland.
